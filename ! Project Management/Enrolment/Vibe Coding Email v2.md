Hi everyone,

As part of our two-day AI executive retreat, we will include a vibe coding session in which each participant builds a simple app with AI support.

In practical terms, vibe coding means describing what you want to build in natural language and using AI to generate, refine, and troubleshoot the code with you. The goal is not to become a software engineer in a day, but to turn ideas into working prototypes quickly.

To make the session more productive, please give some thought in advance to an app you would like to build. It can be work-related or personal, analytical or creative, practical or experimental.

One important guideline: if possible, avoid ideas that depend on storing large amounts of data, managing databases, or building user accounts. Those features are absolutely possible, but they tend to consume a disproportionate amount of session time unless you are already comfortable with cloud tools and app architecture.

Good candidates are apps that:

Solve a specific problem
Analyse or visualise information
Use AI to interpret text or images
Generate recommendations, summaries, or content
Produce something useful within a single session

Examples from previous courses include:

A music app that recommends and plays songs based on mood and a chosen era
An animal spoor identifier based on an uploaded photo
A retro-style game inspired by classic arcade or console formats
A children’s storybook generator with illustrations
A wine or whisky assistant that analyses a label and returns tasting notes, pricing, and pairings
An Excel-to-dashboard prototype that turns monthly reports into a live web view
A recipe generator based on a photo of the contents of a fridge
A photo tagging tool that classifies image libraries by subject matter
A nutrition estimator that analyses a meal from a photo
A medical reference chatbot tailored to country-specific treatment variations
A route planner that maps an efficient delivery sequence for a list of customers
A location-based concierge app that recommends events and activities near a property


Please come with one idea in mind, and ideally a second backup option in case you want to pivot quickly during the session.

We are looking forward to seeing what you build.
