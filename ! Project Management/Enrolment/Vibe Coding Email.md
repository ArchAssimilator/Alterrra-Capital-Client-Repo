In our two-day AI executive retreat, we will be vibe coding an app. It would be helpful if you could pre-think something that you would like to vibe code, and following are some guidelines & ideas:

It can be personal or work related. Technical or soft. Hard numbers or AI generated guidance. 
While storage and databases are easy to implement, they will consume half an hour of your time in the session, and thus we prefer to avoid them unless you are already familiar with cloud databases. So if your app collects data or pictures and keeps a record of them, that's what you should try and avoid for now.
Following is a bunch of ideas that people have successfully coded in the past courses:
Enter your mood, get a playlet of suitable songs, released 1980-1985, and play them, no adverts
Take a photo (or upload) of animal spoor and identify the animal and size.
1980's platform game, or indeed any Spectrum, Commodore, Gameboy, or MAME arcade game you can think of.
Describe a character and scene, and create a storybook with pictures, for a six-year-old.
Rebuild Vivino, the world’s leading wine expertise app; Take a photo of a label or type it in to get a complete description of the wine, pricing and suitable pairings. Also done for whiskey/whisky in a recent course.
Take your monthly Excel report files and turn them into a live web dashboard. Add the ability to switch between months.
Take a photo of your fridge (or upload) and get recipes that can be constructed with the available ingredients.
Take a directory full of photos and have the EXIF data updated to tag specific interests such as people, plants, furniture, etc.
Take a photo of a plate of food and have the calories, macros, and other nutritional info estimated.
A gynaecologist created a translator for peri-menopause therapies, across eight different countries that she covers, where all the drug names, ethical ingredients, and dosing varies. Turned it into a chatbot, all done within one hour.
Input a list of customers and get a Google map showing the optimal route to deliver to all of them. 
A very large South African hotel group, built did an app that lists events and activities in the immediate surrounds of any one of their properties across the world.
