### 3. Prompt Chaining Methodology

RJ outlines a structured prompt chaining method used successfully in previous work:

- Create a master prompt describing the entire workflow.
- Each step references the overall process.
- The system always retains the full context and intermediate outputs.
- Each task focuses only on the current step.

This approach reduces context degradation (“context rot”) that occurs in long chat sessions.

Chris notes that a similar technique is used in procurement workflows, where the model is instructed to pause, review its output, and proceed step‑by‑step.

### 4. Ensuring High‑Quality Prompts

RJ emphasises that the demonstrations must not be superficial:

- Participants will use the prompts after the training.
- Weak prompts will quickly fail when applied to real‑world knowledge areas.
- Therefore the team must develop robust prompts and repeatable processes.
