# DEEP RESEARCH PROMPT: JAVA HOUSE & ARTCAFFE (ChatGPT Deep Research Optimised)

---

### OpenAI Deep Research Prompt

> **Before you submit this prompt:**
> If you have any internal background notes, prior briefing decks, or portfolio-company materials on Java House, Artcaffe, Alterra Capital, ECP, or Abraaj, attach them as supporting documents. If not, run this prompt as-is and prioritise live public sources.

---

**R - Role**

You are a senior private equity research analyst specialising in African consumer, hospitality, and restaurant-platform investments. You are preparing an investment-grade intelligence briefing for a training audience of private equity analysts and principals.

---

**C - Context**

Imagineers.Ai has been retained by Alterra Capital in Nairobi to support a Generative AI training programme for Alterra's investment team.

Two Alterra portfolio companies are participating in the programme:

- **Java House**: Kenya's leading coffee-house and casual dining chain
- **Artcaffe**: a premium cafe, restaurant, and bakery chain in Kenya

The aim is to produce a high-quality outside-in research brief that helps the audience understand:

- each company's business model, scale, and leadership
- the founder stories behind each business
- the private-equity and sponsor lineage behind each company
- how Alterra's current stewardship connects, or does not connect, to earlier ECP / Emerging Capital Partners and Abraaj involvement
- the structure and dynamics of the Kenyan food-service, coffee-house, and QSR market
- how Java House and Artcaffe compete against each other and against other relevant market players

This brief will be used for training, so factual accuracy, source quality, and clean separation between confirmed facts and inference matter more than narrative flourish.

---

**T - Task**

Produce a rigorous intelligence briefing on **Java House** and **Artcaffe** that:

1. profiles each company separately
2. reconstructs each company's founder history and private-equity timeline separately
3. explains the current link to Alterra Capital
4. maps the Kenyan and broader East African food-service landscape
5. compares Java House and Artcaffe head-to-head and against relevant competitors
6. clearly distinguishes between:
   - confirmed facts supported by multiple credible sources
   - plausible but single-sourced claims
   - analyst inference where the public record is incomplete

---

**I - Instructions**

Work in the following order.

**Step 1 - Disentangle the two company histories before doing broad synthesis**

Treat **Java House** and **Artcaffe** as separate research objects unless high-confidence sources prove otherwise. Do not assume they followed the same ownership path merely because both now sit within Alterra's portfolio.

Start by establishing, for each company separately:

- founding date and founder identities
- earliest available ownership history
- major funding, buyout, or control transactions
- current ownership or sponsor stewardship where disclosed

If the Java House and Artcaffe timelines converge only at a later stage, show that explicitly.

**Step 2 - Build a source hierarchy and use it consistently**

Prioritise sources in this order:

1. official company websites, company filings, regulator notices, competition authority approvals, and transaction announcements
2. reputable private-equity, legal, advisory, and lender disclosures
3. major financial and business press
4. sector reports and trade publications
5. interviews, podcasts, and founder profiles

Use lower-tier sources only when higher-tier sources do not exist, and flag that limitation.

**Step 3 - Research each company profile separately**

For **each** company, cover:

- corporate snapshot: legal entities where disclosed, footprint, outlet count, geographies, headcount, revenue / EBITDA proxies, and growth signals
- brand and operating model: market positioning, dayparts, dine-in vs takeaway, delivery, sub-brands, format variations, and any site-economics commentary
- leadership: founder role today, CEO / MD, board or C-suite changes, and management depth where observable
- founders and entrepreneurs: education, nationality, earlier career, prior ventures, motivation for founding the business, current role, and timeline of any sale, dilution, or operational step-back

Do not blend founder biographies across the two businesses.

**Step 4 - Reconstruct the sponsor and transaction history carefully**

For **Java House**, trace the most credible available sequence across:

- ECP / Emerging Capital Partners involvement
- any take-private, control, or platform-building steps
- Abraaj involvement, if evidenced
- consequences of Abraaj's collapse, if evidenced
- transition to Alterra Capital or successor stewardship

For **Artcaffe**, trace its own history separately across:

- founding and early growth
- ECP or other sponsor involvement, if evidenced
- reported approval values, financing structures, mezzanine or co-investors where disclosed
- any later transfer into the current sponsor ecosystem

Do **not** import the Java House / Abraaj narrative into Artcaffe unless directly supported.

For every material transaction, capture where available:

- date
- buyer / seller
- stake size
- reported valuation or deal value
- financing structure if mentioned
- strategic rationale

**Step 5 - Link historical sponsors to current Alterra personnel**

Identify which current Alterra executives, partners, or deal principals appear to have worked on, inherited, or overseen relevant Java House or Artcaffe investments during earlier ECP and/or Abraaj periods.

Where the evidence is indirect, label it clearly as inference rather than fact.

**Step 6 - Analyse industry structure and competitive dynamics**

Map the Kenyan, and where relevant East African, market across:

- market size and growth indicators
- urban consumer demand trends
- premium vs mass-market positioning
- formal chain penetration vs independents
- coffee, bakery, casual dining, and QSR segment dynamics
- supply-chain constraints and input-cost pressures
- real-estate and mall-location considerations
- regulatory considerations such as food safety, imports, employment, VAT, and licensing

Then assess competitive positioning for:

- Java House vs Artcaffe
- Java House vs Big Square, KFC Kenya, Subway, Nairobi Street Kitchen, and other visible competitors
- Artcaffe vs premium cafes, bakeries, and lifestyle-dining competitors

Focus on:

- pricing architecture
- target customer
- menu breadth
- brand perception
- location strategy
- delivery / takeaway relevance
- scalability and defensibility

**Step 7 - Cross-check and grade confidence**

Cross-reference important claims wherever possible. For each major factual claim:

- treat it as **High confidence** if supported by primary sources or multiple credible secondary sources
- treat it as **Medium confidence** if supported by one strong secondary source or partially corroborated evidence
- treat it as **Low confidence** if based on a single weak source, anecdotal reporting, or inference

If a key fact cannot be verified, say so directly instead of smoothing over the gap.

**Step 8 - Optimise for Deep Research output quality**

Keep the analysis focused on research questions that matter to a private equity audience. Avoid generic country summaries and avoid repeating background that does not change the investment interpretation.

Use concise tables where they improve precision, especially for:

- founder timeline
- ownership / transaction timeline
- company comparison
- competitor map
- source log

Write in **UK English** and in clean markdown.

Deliver the final answer in markdown using this structure:

## 1. Executive Summary

Keep this to approximately 1-2 pages. Summarise:

- what matters most about Artcaffe

- key strategic question or risk

## 3. Industry Overview

Cover:

- market structure

- Key open questions

## 5. Company Profile: Artcaffe

## 6. Sponsor Lineage and Timeline Comparison

Create a table showing:

- company
- year

- parties involved

## 7. Competitive Analysis
