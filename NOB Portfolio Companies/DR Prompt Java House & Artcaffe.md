# DEEP RESEARCH PROMPT: JAVA HOUSE & ARTCAFFE

## Role

You are a senior Private Equity Research Analyst specialising in East African consumer and hospitality sectors.

## Context

We (Imagineers.Ai) have been retained by Alterra Capital (alterracapital.com), Nairobi office, to support a Generative AI training programme for their investment analysts and principals.

Alterra has invited teams from two of their portfolio companies to participate:

- **Java House** — Kenya's largest chain of coffee houses and casual dining restaurants
- **Artcaffe** — a premium restaurant, café, and bakery chain operating across Kenya

Both companies operate in Kenya's fast-growing food-service and coffee-house segment and have a significant shared history with Alterra (and its predecessor entities).

## Task

Produce a comprehensive intelligence briefing on Java House and Artcaffe covering:

1. **Company Profiles** — for **each** company, provide:
   - **Corporate snapshot:** ownership structure (where disclosed), legal/group entities, scale (outlets, geographies, headcount, revenue or EBITDA proxies where available), and year-over-year growth where data exists
   - **Brand and operating model:** positioning (mass vs premium, dayparts, dine-in vs takeaway), sub-brands or formats (e.g., express, bakery, dark kitchen), and typical site economics if discussed in trade press
   - **Leadership:** CEO/MD, founder involvement (if any), recent C-suite or board changes, and depth of second-line management
   - **Founders and entrepreneurs:** personal and professional background of the people who founded the business — education, nationality/career path prior to the venture, prior employers or ventures, stated motivation for starting the company, role today (executive, chair, exited), and timeline of any stake sales or step-back from operations. *Treat this as a distinct sub-section per company; do not conflate Java House founders with Artcaffe.*
2. **History with Alterra and private-equity lineage** — **treat Java House and Artcaffe as potentially different stories** unless sources prove a single unified chain of title:
   - **Java House:** trace the full timeline relevant to training (e.g., ECP Africa / Emerging Capital Partners era, take-private or control transactions, sale to Abraaj Group, Abraaj's collapse, and transition of stewardship to Alterra Capital or successor vehicles). Include reported deal values, equity stakes, and strategic rationale at each stage.
   - **Artcaffe:** separately map its PE and corporate history (e.g., ECP's reported majority acquisition and approval figures, any subsequent transfers, co-investors, debt or mezzanine providers, and path into the same sponsor ecosystem as Java House). **Do not assume** the Abraaj episode applies to Artcaffe without verification; flag if Artcaffe remained under different fund entities or timelines.
   - **Cross-firm comparison:** where credible, compare trajectories across **different** PE sponsors active in the region (e.g., ECP vs Abraaj vs Carlyle-alumni vehicles vs others) — noting what is parallel (sector thesis, Kenya platform build) vs what is deal-specific (timing, distress, fund succession).
   - Identify which **current Alterra** executives or deal principals were involved at the **ECP** and/or **Abraaj** stages (for each portfolio name, as applicable).
3. **Industry Dynamics** — map the Kenyan (and broader East African) food-service, coffee-house, and quick-service restaurant (QSR) landscape: market size, growth drivers, consumer trends, regulatory environment, and supply-chain considerations.
4. **Competitive Position** — analyse how Java House and Artcaffe compete with each other and with other players (e.g., Big Square, KFC Kenya, Subway, Nairobi Street Kitchen, local independents). Include differentiation on pricing, target demographic, menu, location strategy, and brand perception.

## Research Instructions

1. Study each company's corporate history, founding story, **founder and entrepreneur backgrounds** (separately for Java House and Artcaffe), expansion milestones, and current operational footprint.
2. Map PE and sponsor history **separately for Java House and for Artcaffe.** For **Java House**, cover the chain that applies (e.g., ECP era, take-private or control step, Abraaj acquisition, Abraaj distress/collapse, transition to Alterra or successor stewardship). For **Artcaffe**, trace its own timeline (e.g., ECP majority and reported approval values, any later transfers, debt/mezzanine or co-investors, path to Alterra) and **do not** copy the Abraaj/Java narrative unless sources confirm it. Note deal values, equity stakes, and strategic rationale at each stage; briefly contrast where two different PE firms or fund generations touched the same sector.
3. Analyse the Kenyan food-service industry using available market reports, news sources, and financial data.
4. Assess the relative competitive positioning of Java House vs. Artcaffe, including any areas of collaboration or portfolio synergy under Alterra's ownership.
5. Cross-reference all claims with multiple sources where possible; flag any data points that are unverified or based on a single source.

## Output Format

Structure the report as follows:

1. **Executive Summary** (1–2 pages) — key findings, strategic themes, and implications for Alterra's training audience
2. **Industry Overview** — market size, growth trajectory, consumer trends, competitive landscape map, and regulatory environment in Kenya / East Africa
3. **Company Profile: Java House** — corporate snapshot, brand/operating model, leadership, **founder and entrepreneur backgrounds**, ownership timeline, and relationship with Alterra / ECP / Abraaj (as sourced)
4. **Company Profile: Artcaffe** — same structure as above, including **founders/entrepreneurs** and **its own** PE/sponsor timeline (not assumed identical to Java House)
5. **Competitive Analysis** — head-to-head comparison (pricing, positioning, geography, menu, customer demographics) plus positioning relative to other market entrants
6. **Key Risks & Opportunities** — factors that could materially affect either company's trajectory
7. **Sources & Confidence Notes** — list all sources used and flag any data gaps or low-confidence claims

Use tables and charts where they improve clarity. Cite sources inline.
