# Basic RCTIO prompt

**Role**
You are an expert prompt engineer and pharmaceutical regulatory compliance advisor.

**Context**
I want to create a reusable prompt for a Gemini Gem or CustomGPT. The tool will help a licensed pharmaceutical distributor in Uganda manage a pharmaceutical product recall from start to finish. It should guide an executive team through the full recall response, using the attached National Drug and Health Products Authority Act as the legal and regulatory reference.

**Task**
Create a detailed, multi-step operational prompt that the Gem or CustomGPT can follow during an active product recall. The prompt should lead the user through the whole process: classifying the recall, gathering product and batch information, assessing risk, identifying customers supplied, notifying the regulator, stopping distribution, quarantining stock, notifying customers, tracking returns, communicating with the manufacturer, preparing public communications, and closing out the recall.

**Instructions**
Make the prompt practical, structured, and action-oriented. It should work in phases, asking all necessary questions at each phase before generating outputs. It should not just give advice, it should generate the actual documents and templates needed during the recall. Use the attached Act to identify legal duties, risks, timelines, responsible persons, regulator notifications, recordkeeping duties, pharmacovigilance obligations, quarantine requirements, and destruction requirements. The tone should be calm, authoritative, and suitable for a senior management team under pressure.

**Output**
Produce a complete system-style prompt for the Gem or CustomGPT. Include the role, context, task, operating instructions, phased workflow, user questions, decision rules, and all required outputs. The final prompt should enable the AI assistant to generate recall documents such as internal alerts, regulator notification letters, customer recall notices, return forms, quarantine labels, tracking logs, manufacturer notices, press releases, and final recall effectiveness reports.

This should demonstrate how a short RCTIO prompt can expand into the much more detailed operational prompt in the attached file, which includes phased recall handling, document generation, NDHPA-specific legal guidance, customer communications, quarantine instructions, tracking logs, press release handling, and close-out reporting.
