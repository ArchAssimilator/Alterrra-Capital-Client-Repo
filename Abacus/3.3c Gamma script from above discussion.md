## Slide 2 — Market Opportunity

**Title:** ~1.75M births/year. Uganda is a real RSV market.

**Subtitle:** Four regions modeled across public/donor and private channels

**Content:**

* National live births: ~145,800/month (~1.75M/year)
* Model covers 4 regions: Central, Western, Eastern, Northern
* Dual channel: Public/Donor-funded clinics + Private providers
* Key gating factor: ANC access rates range from 58% (Northern) to 72% (Central)
* Central region is the largest single opportunity at 28% of national births

## Slide 6 — Cost Structure

**Title:** Fixed costs dominate early. Variable costs scale with volume.

**Subtitle:** Month 1 total costs: Base UGX 130M, Positive UGX 109M. Fixed costs are ~96% of total at launch.

**Content:**

* The cost base is predominantly fixed at launch — regulatory, medical, market access, field reps, office
* Variable costs (vaccine, cold chain, distribution) are modest at low early volumes and grow proportionally
* Positive scenario benefits from leaner launch and training budgets — ~16% lower fixed costs than Base
* As volumes scale, variable costs become more significant but gross margins improve

## Slide 9 — What Drives the Difference

**Title:** The Positive case is not aggressive pricing — it's execution.

**Subtitle:** A UGX 4,527M swing in 36-month cumulative outcome. Driven by access and adoption, not price.

**Content:**

* Price improves only 8% between scenarios — it explains almost none of the gap
* The entire swing comes from three execution levers: eligibility, provider engagement, and rollout speed
* These are not independent — each requires a specific investment or policy commitment
* Month 36 revenue: Base UGX 273M vs Positive UGX 755M — a 2.8× difference on a near-identical cost base

**Dataset — Table: Key Lever Comparison**

| Lever                                    | Base → Positive     | Delta | Investment Required                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Eligibility rate (Central, start)        | 28% → 35%           | +7pp  | Donor/govt programme expansion, NHIS coverage   |
| Provider recommendation (Central, start) | 56% → 63%           | +7pp  | KOL engagement, HCP training, medical education |
| Monthly coverage rollout (Central)       | 2.5% → 3.4%         | +35%  | Facility onboarding, awareness campaigns        |
| Coverage churn (monthly)                 | 0.40% → 0.30%       | −25% | Supply reliability, execution continuity        |
| Net price per dose                       | UGX 35,500 → 38,340 | +8%   | Price is a follower, not a driver               |

**Summary Stats (display as KPI boxes):**

* Month 36 Revenue — Base: **UGX 273M** | Positive: **UGX 755M (+2.8×)**
* 36-Month Cumulative Swing: **UGX 4,527M**

## Slide 10 — Strategic Implications & Next Steps

**Title:** To reach the Positive trajectory, three things must be true.

**Subtitle:** Define the go/no-go criteria and investment required to unlock each condition.

**Content:**

**Three Conditions (display as numbered boxes):**

**① Funding access must expand** Model lever: Eligibility 28% → 35% Action: Confirm NHIS/donor commitment; define coverage expansion roadmap

**② Provider engagement must be sustained** Model lever: Recommendation rate 56% → 63% Action: Approve KOL programme and HCP training investment

**③ Supply chain must be reliable** Model lever: Stock availability 90% → 95% (Central); lower in Northern/Eastern Action: Commit cold-chain and distribution investment, especially Northern and Eastern regions

**Financial Milestones (display as timeline or summary bar):**
