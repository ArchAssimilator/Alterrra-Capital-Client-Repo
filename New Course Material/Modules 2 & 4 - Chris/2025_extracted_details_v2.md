## GROUP PROFILE (Pages 4–12)

### Group Overview
- Famous Brands is **Africa's leading food services franchisor**
- Tagline: "you're in good company"
- Group began as a family business in the early 1960s with the Steers brand
- Current enterprise: vertically integrated business model with **4 522 employees**
- Operations across South Africa, selected African markets, Middle East and United Kingdom
- In November 2024, Famous Brands commemorated **30 years** listed on the JSE (Travel and Leisure sector)
- Business model comprises four core pillars: **Brands, Manufacturing, Logistics, Retail**

### Purpose, Vision and Core Beliefs
- **Purpose:** We nurture our franchise partners to sustainably impact our communities
- **Vision:** To be the leading innovative, branded, franchised and food services business in South Africa and selected markets
- **Strategic intent:** To grow capability and capacity to create great consumer experiences in the branded franchise and food services space
- **Core beliefs:** Humility • Integrity • Agility • Speed • Innovation • Quality • Growth

### 30-Year Listed History (Case Study)
- Famous Brands story originated in the **1960s** when George Halamandres opened the first Steers restaurant
- Business owned and operated by the Halamandres and Halamandris families
- In November 1994, the Group (then known as **Steers Holdings Limited**) initially listed shares at **R1.65 per share**, equating to a market capitalisation of **R41 million**
- At listing, Group managed the Steers brand in SA with limited supply chain
- Group expanded through organic growth and successful acquisitions
- Manufacturing and Logistics operations expanded to offer franchise partners high-quality products at same or lower prices
- Business transitioned from family-owned to listed company; majority of Board now comprises independent non-executive directors
- Group has **1 055 franchise partners** who have been with them for over 30 years, with the longest tenure joining in **1973**
- Several franchise partners have developed multi-brand and multi-restaurant networks
