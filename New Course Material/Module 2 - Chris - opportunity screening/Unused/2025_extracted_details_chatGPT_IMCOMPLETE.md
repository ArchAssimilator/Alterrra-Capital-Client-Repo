#### 30-year listed history case study

* The Famous Brands story began in the **1960s** when **George Halamandres** opened the first Steers restaurant.
* The business was owned and operated by the  **Halamandres and Halamandaris families** .
* In  **November 1994** , the Group, then known as  **Steers Holdings Limited** , offered shares at  **R1.65 per share** .
* This equated to a  **market capitalisation of R41 million** .
* At listing, the Group managed the Steers brand in South Africa with a  **limited supply chain component** .
* After the dawn of democracy in South Africa, the food services category expanded with:
  * strong consumer demand,
  * and growth of the middle class.
* The Group expanded through:
  * strong organic growth,
  * successful acquisitions,
  * and expansion of Manufacturing and Logistics to support franchise partners with high-quality products at the same or lower prices.
* Since listing, the Group has moved from a **family-owned company** to operating in a  **highly regulated listed environment** .
* Governance structures matured over time and new risk management disciplines were introduced.
* Today, the  **majority of the Board comprises independent non-executive directors** .
* The Group says its growth story would not be possible without the commitment of franchise partners.
* It has **1 055 franchise partners** who have journeyed with the Group for over 30 years.
* The longest-tenure franchise partner joined in  **1973** .
* Several franchise partners have developed  **multi-brand and multi-restaurant networks** .
* The case study notes that over 30 years the company has retained:
  * its entrepreneurial spirit,
  * love of innovation,
  * and drive to serve franchise partners and consumers.
