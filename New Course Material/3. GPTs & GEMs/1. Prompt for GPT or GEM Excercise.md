## Task

Identify the **three best exercise options** for the in-class GPT/Gem-building demonstration, drawn from real private equity workflows around acquisition opportunity evaluation.

## Instructions

1. **Read the Famous Brands source material** (paths above) to ground yourself in the specific workflows already demonstrated in this case study.

2. **Research how private equity firms use custom GPTs or Gems** — focus on deal origination, due diligence, IC memo preparation, portfolio monitoring, and LP reporting.

3. **Generate a longlist of at least 10 candidate exercise options.** For each, consider:
   - Is it grounded in a real PE workflow that participants will recognise?
   - Can participants configure and test the GPT/Gem in under 20 minutes?
   - Does the output clearly demonstrate the *value-add* of a customised AI vs. a plain chat prompt?
   - Does it connect naturally to the Famous Brands / take-private case study?
   - Does it require a meaningful system prompt + knowledge upload (not just a chat tweak)?

4. **Score and rank all 10+ options** against those five criteria. Be explicit about trade-offs.

5. **Present the top 3**, with a short build-spec for each so a participant could actually construct it in class.

## Output Format

For each of the top 3 options, provide:

**Option [N]: [Name]**

- **PE workflow it replicates:** Where in the deal lifecycle this sits
- **Famous Brands angle:** How it ties to the take-private case study
- **System prompt skeleton:** The core instruction block a participant would paste in
- **Knowledge base:** What documents/data to upload (reference files from the case where applicable)
- **Demo output:** What the GPT/Gem produces that will impress the room
- **Build time estimate:** Realistic time to configure and test in class
