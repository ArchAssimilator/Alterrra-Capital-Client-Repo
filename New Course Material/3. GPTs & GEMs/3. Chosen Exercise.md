Opportunity Triage Analyst

**One-line pitch:**
A GPT/Gem configured to screen any new investment opportunity using a firm's proprietary triage framework — returning a structured pass/flag/progress recommendation in under 60 seconds.

**PE workflow it replicates:**
Deal origination and first-pass screening. Before a deal reaches an analyst's desk for real work, it must pass a rapid relevance filter. In practice this is done by a senior partner who has internalised the firm's thesis, sector focus, size criteria, and red-line exclusions. A custom GPT encodes that filter and applies it consistently at scale.
