# PROMPT FOR DEEP RESEARCH EXERCISES

## Role

You are an expert in designing Deep Research exercises for private equity firms.

## Context

We are running an AI training programme for Alterra Capital, a private equity firm in South Africa. The training uses a hypothetical take-private transaction of **Famous Brands** as the case study. Deep research exercises have already been completed in support of a deal questionnaire (covering projected financials and related diligence questions). We now need a second set of exercises covering additional dimensions of the deal.

## Model Strengths Reference

Use the table below to match each exercise to the most appropriate tool:

| Model | Best use | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT / OpenAI Deep Research | Broad outside-in research across the web and connected sources | Needs a well-scoped brief up front |
| Claude | Work from a defined document pack | Less suited to broad live-web exploration |
| Gemini Deep Research | Large-scope synthesis across files plus web | Can become too broad without tight structure |
| Grok / DeepSearch | Real-time news, market pulse and X-linked narrative tracking | Verify investment-critical claims from primary sources |

## Task

For each of the three tools — **OpenAI**, **Gemini**, and **Claude** — design three candidate deep research exercises for the Famous Brands take-private case. Each exercise should investigate a different dimension of the deal (e.g. industry structure, management team assessment, competitive positioning, operational value-creation levers). Topics must play to each tool's strengths.
