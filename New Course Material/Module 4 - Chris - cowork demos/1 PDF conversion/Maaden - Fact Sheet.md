## Company Overview

Ma'aden is a leading mining and metals company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with a diverse portfolio of mineral assets at various stages of development including exploration, development, and production. Ma'aden has a broad commodity focus spanning:

- Gold
- Zinc
- Phosphate
- Aluminum
- Industrial minerals

Ma'aden is continually assessing opportunities to enter into new commodities that demonstrate strong long-term fundamentals and opportunities for growth, in line with the company's strategy.

## Key Observations

- **Revenue declined significantly** from SAR 40.3 billion in 2022 to SAR 29.3 billion in 2023 (a ~27% decrease), reflecting lower commodity prices and/or production volumes.
- **Net profit fell sharply** from SAR 12.1 billion to SAR 1.7 billion in 2023 (an ~86% decrease), indicating significant margin compression.
- **Total assets remained relatively stable** at approximately SAR 111.9 billion in 2023 (vs SAR 111.6 billion in 2022), suggesting no major asset disposals.
- **Cash position improved** from SAR 6.1 billion to SAR 10.3 billion, indicating strong cash generation or asset monetisation despite lower profits.
- **Leverage remained broadly stable**, with total liabilities decreasing slightly from SAR 55.5 billion to SAR 55.1 billion.
