# 10 Cold Email Templates to Break the Ice With Funders

*Published by Instrumentl — www.instrumentl.com*

---

## Introduction

We understand the challenges of crafting compelling cold emails that resonate with busy funders while conveying your nonprofit's mission and impact.

That's why we've curated a collection of ten cold email templates, each designed to approach funders from different angles — from emotionally resonant narratives to data-driven approaches. Drawn from proven copywriting techniques and grant writing fundamentals.

Use these templates to:

- **Engage Funders:** Captivate attention with compelling storytelling and strategic messaging.
- **Build Relationships:** Initiate authentic connections that go beyond transactional interactions.
- **Showcase Impact:** Highlight your nonprofit's achievements and the tangible change you bring.
- **Personalize Outreach:** Choose the template that resonates most with your nonprofit's ethos.
- **Elevate Conversations:** Foster meaningful discussions that can lead to fruitful partnerships.

> *Tip: Copy and paste any template into a Google Doc and edit it directly. The key to successful cold emailing lies in the delicate balance between persuasion and authenticity.*

## Template 1: Storytelling Approach

**Subject:** Transforming [Beneficiary's Name]'s Life – A Hope You Can Support!

Dear [Receiver Name],

I hope this email finds you well.

Let me share a story that touched my heart and will perhaps touch yours too. Meet [Beneficiary's Name]. At just [age], [he/she] faced [specific challenge], an ordeal most of us couldn't even imagine. But here's where the magic lies: with the right support, [his/her] story could be one of triumph over adversity.

Our organization, [Your Nonprofit's Name], aims to rewrite the narrative for individuals like [Beneficiary's Name]. Over the past [number of years in operation], we've transformed the lives of [number of beneficiaries], but we're just scratching the surface.

I genuinely believe that a collaboration between our vision and [their organization's name]'s philanthropic goals could spark incredible change. Would you be open to a brief discussion on how your funding can make an immense difference?

Your potential partnership could be the beacon of hope for many waiting for their story to change.

Regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name] | [Contact Information]

## Template 2: Value Proposition Approach

**Subject:** Partnering for Impact: Join [Your Nonprofit's Name] in Changing Lives!

Dear [Receiver Name],

Greetings from [Your Nonprofit's Name]!

I'm reaching out with a unique proposition, one that aligns with our shared vision of [insert common mission]. [Your organization's name], in its [number of years in operation], has achieved [specific accomplishment]. Yet, the journey ahead still holds many more opportunities to make a tangible difference.

Here's where your help can make a huge difference:

1. **Proven Impact:** We've successfully [specific achievement] with a lean model, ensuring every dollar spent delivers on our promise.
2. **Transparent Processes:** With regular updates, audits, and reports, you'll witness your contribution's transformational journey.
3. **Tailored Partnerships:** We value collaboration and are eager to discuss bespoke partnership structures that resonate with your philanthropic objectives.

We're inviting you to be an integral part of our mission. Would you consider a brief chat to explore this potential synergy?

Best,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name] | [Contact Information]

## Template 3: Empathy & Urgency Approach

**Subject:** Every Moment Counts: [Beneficiary's Challenge]

Dear [Receiver Name],

Every day, [X number of beneficiaries] in our community face [specific challenge]. For individuals like [specific case or beneficiary name], this isn't just a number; it's a grim reality that limits their future potential.

At [Your Nonprofit's Name], we've successfully:

- Launched [specific program],
- Supported [Y number of beneficiaries] in the past [Z years],
- Achieved a [specific result].

Yet, the need grows every day. We're reaching out, hoping that with your backing, we can expand our initiatives and become the lifeline these individuals desperately seek.

Can we explore a partnership, where together, we respond to this pressing call?

Best Regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name] | [Contact Information]

## Template 5: Data-Driven Approach

**Subject:** Beyond Stories: The Tangible Impact of [Your Nonprofit's Name]

Dear [Receiver Name],

While stories tug at our heartstrings, numbers showcase tangible impact. Here's a snapshot of our work at [Your Nonprofit's Name]:

- [X number of beneficiaries] directly supported in the past year,
- A [Y% increase] in [specific result],
- Every dollar invested yields [Z value] of community benefit.

Your esteemed contribution could propel these figures, ensuring that every cent is translated into transformative societal value.

Keen to dive deeper into this data journey with us?

Warm Regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name] | [Contact Information]

## Template 6: Collaborative Approach

**Subject:** Crafting Impact Together: An Invitation to Collaborate

Dear [Receiver Name],

Philanthropy isn't a solo journey; it thrives on collaboration. At [Your Nonprofit's Name], we've:

- Successfully collaborated with [X number of organizations],
- Co-created [Y initiatives],
- Fostered a network of [Z number of stakeholders] dedicated to change.

We're excited about the idea of having [Funder's Organization Name] on board. Imagine the change we could drive, harnessing our combined expertise and passion.

How about a meeting to brainstorm synergistic opportunities?

Sincerely,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name] | [Contact Information]

## Template 7: Common Ground Approach

Best,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name]

## Template 8: Mutual Acquaintance Approach

**Subject:** [Mutual Contact's Name] Mentioned We Should Chat!

Dear [Receiver Name],

Recently, over coffee, [Mutual Contact's Name] and I discussed some of our work at [Your Nonprofit's Name]. They mentioned you have a keen interest in [related field or cause].

I thought it might be great to connect, share stories, and learn from each other. Would you be interested in connecting over coffee sometime in the next couple of weeks? [Insert availability]

Cheers,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name]

## Template 9: Curiosity & Learning Approach

**Subject:** Seeking Insights on [Relevant Topic]

Dear [Receiver Name],

I've been on a journey, exploring [specific topic], and your name keeps popping up linked with some insightful work in this realm.

I'd love to learn from you, exchange ideas, and get some insights. No specific agenda, just two enthusiasts chatting about a cause close to their hearts. Does that sound appealing?

Warmly,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name]

## Template 10: Shared Event/Experience Approach

**Subject:** Connecting After [Event/Experience]

Dear [Receiver Name],

I noticed you were also part of [Event/Experience] last month. It's a shame our paths didn't cross there! I was representing [Your Nonprofit's Name], and the experiences shared were profoundly enlightening.

I'd love to connect, discuss some of the key takeaways, and hear your thoughts. Fancy a casual catch-up over coffee or a virtual call?

Regards,
[Your Name] | [Your Nonprofit's Name]
