Introduction
We understand the challenges of crafting compelling cold emails that resonate with busy
funders while conveying your nonprofit's mission and impact.
That's why we've curated a collection of ten cold email templates, each designed to approach
funders from different angles.
The templates range from emotionally resonant narratives to data-driven approaches.
We've drawn inspiration from proven copywriting techniques and grant writing fundamentals,
ensuring that each template is not only persuasive but also tailor-made for forging meaningful
connections.
Whether you're looking to make a direct impact, establish common ground, or foster curiosity,
our templates have you covered.
Use these templates to:
Engage Funders: Captivate funders' attention with compelling storytelling and strategic
messaging.
Build Relationships: Initiate authentic connections that go beyond transactional
interactions.
Showcase Impact: Highlight your nonprofit's achievements and the tangible change you
bring.
Personalize Outreach: Choose the template that resonates most with your nonprofit's
ethos.
Elevate Conversations: Foster meaningful discussions that can lead to fruitful partnerships.
Simply personalize the template with your nonprofit's details and start reaching out with
confidence.
Remember, the key to successful cold emailing lies in the delicate balance between persuasion
and authenticity.
Happy emailing!
The Instrumentl Team

Storytelling Approach
TO:
Receiver Name Month DD, YYYY
Company name / seat
123 Anywhere St., Any City, ST 12345
Subject
Subject: Transforming [Beneficiary's Name]'s Life – A Hope You Can Support!
Dear Receiver Name
I hope this email finds you well.
Let me share a story that touched my heart and will perhaps touch yours too. Meet
[Beneficiary's Name]. At just [age], [he/she] faced [specific challenge], an ordeal most of us
couldn't even imagine. But here's where the magic lies: with the right support, [his/her] story
could be one of triumph over adversity.
Our organization, [Your Nonprofit's Name], aims to rewrite the narrative for individuals like
[Beneficiary's Name]. Over the past [number of years in operation], we've transformed the lives
of [number of beneficiaries], but we're just scratching the surface.
I genuinely believe that a collaboration between our vision and [their organization's name]
philanthropic goals could spark incredible change. Would you be open to a brief discussion on
how your funding can make an immense difference?
Your potential partnership could be the beacon of hope for many waiting for their story to
change.
Regards,
Name Surname

Value Proposition Approach
TO:
Receiver Name Month DD, YYYY
Company name / seat
123 Anywhere St., Any City, ST 12345
Subject
Partnering for Impact: Join [Your Nonprofit's Name] in Changing Lives!
Dear Receiver Name
Greetings from [Your Nonprofit's Name]!
I'm reaching out with a unique proposition, one that aligns with our shared vision of [insert
common mission]. [Your organization’s name], in its [number of years in operation], has
achieved [specific accomplishment].
Yet, the journey ahead still holds many more opportunities to make a tangible difference.
Here's where your help can make a huge difference:
1.Proven Impact: We've successfully [specific achievement, e.g., "rehabilitated 500 homeless
individuals"] with a lean model, ensuring every dollar spent delivers on ou promise.
2.Transparent Processes: With regular updates, audits, and reports, you'll witness your
contribution's transformational journey.
3.Tailored Partnerships: We value collaboration and are eager to discuss bespoke
partnership structures that resonate with your philanthropic objectives.
We're inviting you to be an integral part of our mission. Would you consider a brief chat to
explore this potential synergy?
Thank you for your time, and I eagerly await your thoughts.
Best, [Your Name] [Your Nonprofit's Name] [Contact Information]
Regards,
Name Surname

Empathy & Urgency Approach

Company name / seat
123 Anywhere St., Any City, ST 12345
Subject
Every Moment Counts: [Beneficiary's Challenge, e.g., "Children Without Education"]
Dear Receiver Name
Every day, [X number of beneficiaries, e.g., "200 children"] in our community face [specific
challenge, e.g., "a life without access to basic education"]. For individuals like [specific case or
beneficiary name], this isn’t just a number; it's a grim reality that limits their future potential.
At [Your Nonprofit's Name], we've successfully:
Launched [specific program, e.g., "an after-school education center"],
Supported [Y number of beneficiaries, e.g., "over 1,000 children"] in the past [Z years, e.g.,
"3 years"],
Achieved a [specific result, e.g., "90% success rate in helping children transition to regular
schools"].
Yet, the need grows every day. We're reaching out, hoping that with your backing, we can
expand our initiatives and become the lifeline these individuals desperately seek.
Can we explore a partnership, where together, we respond to this pressing call?
Best Regards,
[Your Name] [Your Nonprofit's Name] [Contact Information]
