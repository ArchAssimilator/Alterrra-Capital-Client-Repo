## Meeting A — Wednesday, 10 June 2020 (15:05 UTC)

The Chair (CB) welcomed members present and opened the meeting at 15:05 UTC. A roll call confirmed quorum. SM and SI declared conflict of interest on Agenda Items 4 and 7 (Nomination Committee and AGMM 2020).

### 2. Adoption of the Agenda

The Agenda was adopted as circulated. *Proposed: SO. Seconded: HY.*

### 3. Approval of Previous Minutes

**3.1 Minutes dated 12 & 19 February 2020**

> **Resolution 202006.554** — The Board approved the minutes of the Board meetings held on 12 and 19 February 2020 with amendments. *Proposed: HY. Seconded: SI.*

*Note: The Chair CB was disconnected at 15:12 UTC; the Vice-Chair led the meeting.*

**3.2 Minutes dated 20 March 2020**

> **Resolution 202006.554** — The Board approved the minutes of the Board meeting held on 20 March 2020 with amendments. *Proposed: SO. Seconded: HY.*

**3.3 Matters Arising:** None.

*CB rejoined at 15:36 UTC.*

### 4. Committees' Reports

**4.1 Remuneration Committee**

The Board took note of the report of the Remuneration Committee. It was noted that some recommendations to the Board are to be updated before proceeding with the request for board approval.

> **Action Item 202006.01:** The REMCO to finalise the items/policies that require Board approval; to update the recommendations section of its report and revert back by 17 June 2020.

**4.2 Audit Committee**

The Chair of the Audit Committee presented its report. The Whistleblower mechanism has been implemented and the URL is available on the AFRINIC website.

**4.2.1 Audited Financial Statements**

The Board was informed that the auditor will not sign off the audited financial statements subject to receipt of the KYC clearance letter from the MCB. The level of bad debts is increasing at a higher rate compared to the previous year.

> **Action Item 202006.02:** The AuditCo to share the Business Continuity Plan by 17 June 2020 to the Board for approval.

> **Action Item 202006.03:** The AuditCo to submit the name of the external Auditor to be appointed by the next board meeting.

**4.3 Finance Committee**

The Chair of the Finance Committee presented its report. The Committee recommended the approval of the Contingency Policy.

> **Resolution 202006.555** — The Board approved the Contingency Policy, which shall take effect from the date of this approval. *Proposed: SM. Seconded: HY. Passed unanimously.*

> **Action Item 202006.04:** The Secretariat to transfer the Contingency Policy into the appropriate template.

> **Action Item 202006.05:** All KYC documents to be submitted to the Secretariat by 10th July.

**4.4 Technical Committee**

The Chair of TechCo presented its report. The Board was informed that the Service Level Committee and Performance Level Matrix will be considered in the next committee meeting. Management to incorporate the IT Roadmap for the next 4 years into the Strategic Plan.

**4.5 Governance Committee**

The Board Liaison (SO) briefed on the status of work on the bylaws review at GovCom level. Requests for comments have been sent to specific groups of stakeholders; subsequent meetings will be held to review comments received.

*CB handed over Chairmanship to HY at 17:34 UTC. SI and SM left at 17:34 UTC.*

**4.6 Nomination Committee**

The Board Liaison (AA) briefed that NomCom is still at the evaluation stage of applications received. The call for nominations was closed and reopened on several occasions to attract more potential candidates.

*CB resumed the Chairmanship at 17:39 UTC.*

**4.7 Reform Committee**

The Board Liaison (HY) made an overview of the different Working Groups within the ReformCo and the status of work. The work is ongoing.

### 5. Resolution passed by Circulation

**5.1 Internet Access**

> **Resolution 202005.553** — Directors be exceptionally paid an amount not exceeding **USD 200 monthly** for Internet/Communication Allowance claims for the period starting 01 March 2020 up to 30 September 2020 (or until such period as the Board may decide in light of the evolution of the COVID-19 pandemic). The Board may review the aforesaid quantum from time to time. *Proposed: CB. Seconded: SO. Approved by email dated 28 May 2020.*

*Note: One Board Member voted No, and another member abstained to the resolution. Both members declined the increase.*

### 13. NRO INRS JPAA

The CEO briefed on the NRO Memorandum of Understanding – Internet Number Registry System Joint Project Agreement Addendum and sought the approval of the Board to sign the MoU on behalf of AFRINIC.

> **Resolution 202006.557** — The Board authorized the CEO to sign the addendum to the NRO Memorandum of Understanding – Internet Number Registry System Joint Project Agreement Addendum (NRO INRS JPAA) on behalf of African Network Information Centre Ltd. *Proposed: SM. Seconded: HY. Passed unanimously.*

*The Chair adjourned the meeting at 17:54 UTC. The meeting was to reconvene on Wednesday 17 June 2020.*

## Meeting B — Wednesday, 17 June 2020 (15:03 UTC)

The Chair welcomed members present and opened the meeting at 15:03 UTC. A roll call confirmed quorum.

### 4. Committees' Reports (Continued)

**4.1 Remuneration Committee (Continued)**

The REMCO Chair presented the updated report of the Committee. The Committee recommended to recruit a Chief Operations Officer and a Head of IT as proposed by the CEO. The Board approved to recruit a Chief Operations Officer and for REMCO to oversee the recruitment process.

> **Action Item 202006.06:** The Remuneration Committee to draft the Terms of Reference and submit to the Board for approval by the first week of July.

**4.4 Technical Committee (Continued)**

The TechCo Chair briefed on the readjustment between the MyAfrinic and RPKI projects. The RPKI project was recommended for Board approval.

> **Resolution 202006.558** — The Board resolved to reallocate a sum of **USD 71,217** from the Capital Expenses budget (and, where applicable, from the Infrastructure budget) to the RPKI project. *Proposed: VM. Seconded: SM. Passed unanimously.*

Background: The 2020 Capex budget had a provision for the MyAfrinic v2 project, which has been delayed. The RPKI project (total cost: USD 71,217) can be accommodated within the reallocated funds.

**6. CEO Report**

The CEO presented his report to the Board as circulated. The CEO also provided an update on the IP Investigation and the ongoing Database Accuracy project.

**6.1 ZANOG Request**

The Board discussed a request received from ZANOG for a discount. The present discount policy at AFRINIC does not cover such a category. The Board resolved to reconsider and finalise a discount policy/framework to address any such requests in the future.

> **Action Item 202006.07:** The Management and the Finance Committee to review the discount policy and submit to the next Board meeting for consideration.

*The Chair adjourned the meeting at 16:49 UTC to reconvene on Wednesday 24 June 2020 at 15:00 UTC.*

## Meeting C — Wednesday, 24 June 2020 (15:00 UTC)

### 6.2 MCB Resolution — Bank Signatories

> **Resolution 202006.559** — The present bank signatories were revoked and the following persons were appointed as bank signatories of the Company for accounts held at Mauritius Commercial Bank Ltd:

| Name | Position | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Mr Christian Domilongo Bope | AFRINIC Board Chairperson | A |
| Mr Subramanian Moonesamy | AFRINIC Board Vice Chairperson | A |
| Mr Eddy Mabano Kayihura | Chief Executive Officer | A |
| Mr Nirmal Manic | Chief Accountant | A |
| Mr Yavisht Toolseeya | HR & Administration Manager | B |

**Mode of signatures:** Either two "A" Signatories OR one "A" Signatory and one "B" Signatory; with the exception of the Cash Reserves Accounts which shall require three signatories (CEO, Chief Accountant, and either the Chairman or Vice Chairperson). Any transaction in excess of USD 100,000 must be approved by the Board.

**Internet Banking Access** was also updated, with the above-named individuals plus Mr Ganessen Gurunaden (Initiate/View rights only) being granted access to all MCB accounts.

*Proposed: EK. Seconded: CB. Resolution passed.*

### 7. AGMM2020

The Board took note of legal advice regarding holding a virtual AGMM in September 2020 (the law in Mauritius has been amended to make provisions for the post-COVID situation, allowing an AGMM 9 months before the balance sheet date). The Legal Counsel has sought confirmation from the Registrar of Companies and is awaiting a reply.

The CEO recommended using the **Meetecho** online platform to host the event (ZOOM is not available in some African countries). Selected time per day is reduced to 4 hours.

The Board approved to hold the AFRINIC Meeting/AGMM2020 virtually during the week starting **7 September 2020**.

> **Action Item 202006.08:** Management to do testing on Meetecho with the members.

> **Action Item 202006.09:** Management to seek public consultation with Members on the challenges with holding elections for PDP and NRO ASO AC.

> **Action Item 202006.10:** The Chair to communicate to Members the date of the next AGMM2020 by 27 June 2020.

### 8. Approval of Audited Financial Statements 2019

Bope) were authorised to sign the Financial Statements and the associated Letter of Representation on behalf of the Board. *Proposed: AA. Seconded: HY. Resolution passed.*

### 10. AFRINIC Strategic Plan

The Board took note of the amended timeline for drafting the Strategic Plan and the start of the consultation process with different stakeholders. A Board Strategic Plan Retreat is proposed for online in August 2020, with a draft Strategic Plan to be presented to members during the AFRINIC meeting in September 2020.

> **Action Item 202006.11:** The Secretariat to circulate a doodle poll for hosting an online Board Strategic Plan Retreat in August 2020.

### 11. Business Impact of COVID-19

The CEO briefed the Board on the identified business impact of COVID-19 on the company, particularly on the level of surplus. The Chair referred the matter to the Finance Committee for further discussion.

> **Action Item 202006.12:** The Finance Committee to consider the 'Business Impact of COVID-19' on the company and to report back to the Board.

### 12. Alignment of Appeal Committee ToR with PDP

The Board considered feedback received on the Appeal Committee's Terms of Reference with the PDP. The Chair proposed referring the Terms of Reference for public consultation for review and amendments pursuant to the feedback received after the last two appeal cases.

> **Action Item 202006.13:** A public consultation for the Appeal Committee's Terms of Reference to be opened for a period of one month.

### 15. AoB

**15.1 Court Injunction Against AFRINIC**

The CEO updated the Board on a Court Injunction against AFRINIC. It was agreed to hold a Special Board meeting for further discussion.

> **Action Item 202006.14:** The Secretariat to circulate a doodle poll to convene a Special Board Meeting on Mon 29th or Tues 30th June 2020 with respect to the Court Injunction against AFRINIC.

**15.2 CEO Reporting to the Board**

One member proposed that a proper reporting mechanism should be established for the CEO to the Board. It is proposed that the CEO should report to the Chairman and the Chairman should refer matters to the Board, or the Chair should allow the CEO to send urgent matters directly to the Board or redirect to the relevant Committee.
