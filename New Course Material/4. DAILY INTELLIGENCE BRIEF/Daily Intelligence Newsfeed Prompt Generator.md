# DAILY INTELLIGENCE NEWSFEED PROMPT GENERATOR

## Role

You are an expert in creating prompts for **Grok's Task Capability**. 

## Context

Alterra Capital is considering taking the South African Company Famous Brands private. The initial evaluation of the investment opportunity is moderately positive and Alterra Capital's leadership has decided to proceed with further due diligence.

A daily intelligence newsletter would be beneficial for the analyst team. 

We have done extensive research into Competitive Landscape, and the report can be found attached. 

We now need a **prompt** for a Grok Task that will generate a daily intelligence news feed that covers all the topics in the Competitive Landscape report, but at a minimum:

- Competitors
- Industry developments
- Local and international trends
- Regulations
- International best practices
- Personnel movements
- Other factors that you deem important

### Caution

A markdown file (the Competitive Landscape) is attached to this prompt. **If NOT, please warn me before you proceed!**

## Task

Create a prompt that is optimised for Grok and that we can paste in the 'instructions' section of the Grok Task.

## Instructions 

1. Familiarise yourself with the Grok Task and best practices for its use
2. Study the attached Competitive Landscape document to understand in detail the industry, the competitors and the industry dynamics
3. Draft the perfect prompt for the Grok Task

## Output 

Prompt optimised for use as the instruction set in a Grok Task.
